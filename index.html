🛣️ What is a School Street?

A School Street is a road outside a school with a temporary restriction on motorised traffic at school drop-off and pick-up times. The restriction applies to school traffic and through traffic. The result is a safer, healthier and pleasant environment for everyone.

📋 Duty Rules & Guidelines

🚦 On Duty

  • Arrive 5 minutes early to collect the barrier and high-visibility vest from the school office.
  • Position yourself at the designated crossing point — do not move the barrier without permission.
  • Engage the barrier only when it is safe to stop traffic (no oncoming vehicles in sight).
  • Maintain the crossing for the full 30-minute slot until your relief arrives.
  • Never leave the post unattended. If you must leave early, contact the admin immediately.
  • Wear the high-visibility vest at all times while on duty.

📞 How to Communicate

  • Can't make your shift? Update your availability at least 24 hours in advance using the link on the home page.
  • Last-minute emergency? Contact the admin directly by phone/text (details in your confirmation email).
  • Incident on duty? Report it to the school office immediately and submit a written summary by email within 24 hours.
  • New to the role? Ask to shadow an experienced volunteer for your first shift — just let the admin know.
  • Do not engage with the media on behalf of the school. Refer any enquiries to the school office.

⚠️ Safety Rules

  • Never step into the road yourself — your role is to control the barrier, not to direct traffic.
  • In severe weather (thunderstorm, ice), do not proceed without authorisation from the school.
  • If a driver behaves aggressively, do not engage. Note the vehicle and report to the school office.
  • Keep mobile phone use to a minimum while on duty.
  • Children must not cross until the barrier is fully extended and traffic has stopped.

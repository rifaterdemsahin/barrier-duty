Could You Spare 30 Minutes?

We Need Barrier Duty Volunteers!

Every school day, parent volunteers help keep our children safe at the school crossing. It only takes 30 minutes per shift — and it makes a huge difference!

✅ What's Involved?

  • ✅ No experience needed — full training and a welcome walk-through provided
  • ✅ High-visibility vest and barrier supplied by the school
  • ✅ Flexible scheduling — choose shifts that suit you
  • ✅ Join a friendly team of parent volunteers
  • ✅ Make a real, visible difference to your child's daily safety

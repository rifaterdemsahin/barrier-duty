# Volunteer Quick Reference Guide

## 🚧 Barrier Duty Volunteer System - How to Use

Welcome to the Barrier Duty Volunteer Management System! This guide will help you understand how to use the system and update your availability.

---

## Accessing the System

**Main Website:** https://rifaterdemsahin.github.io/barrier-duty/

The website has several sections:
- **Home** - Welcome and quick information
- **Volunteer Rota** - See the schedule for all shifts
- **Volunteers** - View all active volunteers
- **Updates** - Read important announcements
- **Admin** - For administrators only (password protected)

---

## Understanding the Schedule

### Shift Times

**Morning Shift:** 8:00 AM - 8:30 AM  
**Afternoon Shift:** 3:00 PM - 3:30 PM

Each shift is 30 minutes at the school crossing.

### Status Colors

- 🟢 **Confirmed** (Green) - Volunteer has confirmed they can make it
- 🟡 **Pending** (Yellow) - Waiting for volunteer confirmation
- 🔴 **Cancelled** (Red) - Shift cancelled (weather, holiday, etc.)
- ⚪ **Completed** (Gray) - Shift already happened

---

## Email Notifications You'll Receive

### 1. Assignment Notification
**When:** You're assigned to a new shift  
**Subject:** "Action Required: Confirm Your Barrier Duty for [Date]"  
**What to do:** 
- Click the "Update My Availability" button in the email
- Confirm you can make it, or let us know if you can't

### 2. Reminder Email
**When:** Daily reminder if you have unconfirmed shifts  
**Subject:** "Reminder: Barrier Duty Confirmation Needed"  
**What to do:**
- Review your assigned shifts
- Confirm or update your availability

### 3. Confirmation Email
**When:** After you submit an availability update  
**Subject:** "Confirmation: Availability Update Received"  
**What to do:**
- Keep this for your records
- An admin will process your update within 24 hours

## How to Update Your Availability

### Method 1: From Email Link (Easiest)

1. Open the email you received
2. Click "Update My Availability" button
3. The form will be pre-filled with your information
4. Select why you can't make it:
   - **I'm Unavailable** - You can't make this shift
   - **Request a Substitute** - You need someone to cover
   - **Change Request** - You want to change to a different date/time
5. Add a reason (optional but helpful)
6. Click "Submit Update"
7. You'll receive a confirmation email

### Method 2: From Website

1. Go to https://rifaterdemsahin.github.io/barrier-duty/
2. Click "Update My Availability" button on the home page
3. Or scroll to the Volunteer Rota section and click the update link
4. Fill in the form with:
   - Your full name
   - Your email address
   - Update type
   - Date range
   - Reason (optional)
5. Submit the form
6. You'll receive a confirmation email

## Updating Your Availability - Step by Step

### If You Can't Make a Shift

**Step 1:** Click the update link (from email or website)

**Step 2:** Fill in the form:
- **Your Name:** [Your full name]
- **Email:** [Your email address]
- **Update Type:** Select "I'm Unavailable"
- **From Date:** [The date you can't make it]
- **To Date:** [Usually the same date, unless multiple days]
- **Reason:** Optional - helps us understand (e.g., "Doctor appointment", "Out of town", "Family emergency")

**Step 3:** Click "Submit Update"

**Step 4:** Wait for confirmation
- You'll get an email immediately confirming we received your update
- An admin will review and update the schedule
- We'll find a substitute or adjust the schedule
- You'll get another email once it's processed

### If You Need a Substitute

**Step 1:** Follow the same process as above

**Step 2:** Select "Request a Substitute" as the update type

**Step 3:** In the reason field, add any details:
- How urgent is it?
- Do you have someone in mind?
- Can you swap with someone?

**Step 4:** We'll work to find coverage and let you know

### If You're Available (When Not Assigned)

**Step 1:** Use the update form

**Step 2:** Select "I'm Available" as the update type

**Step 3:** Specify the dates you're available

**Step 4:** We'll consider you for upcoming shifts

## Important Guidelines

### ⏰ Timing

- **Confirm or decline** assigned shifts within 48 hours
- If we don't hear from you, we assume you're confirmed
- Give us **at least 24 hours notice** if you can't make it
- For emergencies, contact the admin directly

### 📧 Email

- Make sure your email address is correct
- Check spam folder if you don't receive confirmations
- Keep confirmation emails for your records
- Don't reply to automated emails - use the update form instead

### 🤝 Being a Good Volunteer

- Confirm your shifts promptly
- Give advance notice when you can't make it
- Help find a substitute if possible
- Arrive 5 minutes early for your shift
- Thank you for keeping our children safe! 🚸

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I know which shifts I'm assigned to?

**A:** Check the Volunteer Rota on the website, or look for email notifications about your assignments.

### Q: Can I update multiple dates at once?

**A:** Yes! Use the "From Date" and "To Date" fields to specify a date range.

### Q: What if I have a last-minute emergency?

**A:** Use the update form immediately, and also text/call the admin directly if it's within 24 hours of your shift.

### Q: Can I see who else is volunteering?

**A:** Yes! Check the "Volunteers" section on the website to see all active volunteers.

### Q: How do I sign up for more shifts?

**A:** Use the update form and select "I'm Available" as the update type, then specify which days you're available.

### Q: What if I never received an assignment email?

**A:** Check the website's Volunteer Rota to see if you're scheduled. If you are but didn't get an email, contact the admin.

### Q: Can I swap shifts with another volunteer?

**A:** Yes! Coordinate with the other volunteer, then both of you submit update forms mentioning the swap in the reason field.

### Q: How far in advance is the schedule published?

**A:** Usually 2-4 weeks in advance. Check the Updates section for schedule announcements.

### Q: What should I bring to my shift?

**A:** Just yourself! A high-visibility vest will be provided. Dress for the weather.

### Q: What if there's bad weather?

**A:** Check your email on the morning of your shift. Cancellations are sent early if weather is severe.

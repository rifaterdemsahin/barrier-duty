# 🔍 1_Real_Unknown — The Problem

> Start here. Define the problem you don't yet understand.

## 🎯 OKRs

### Objective
Ensure every barrier duty shift at the school crossing is covered by a trained volunteer, every school day.

### Key Results
- **KR1:** 100% shift coverage rate (currently ~98%) — zero uncovered crossings
- **KR2:** Average response time to fill a vacant slot drops below 2 hours
- **KR3:** At least 30 active registered volunteers on the rota
- **KR4:** All volunteers have completed the onboarding walkthrough before their first shift

---

## ❓ The Questions We're Trying to Answer

1. How do we make it easy for volunteers to see, claim, and manage their shifts without an admin bottleneck?
2. How do volunteers quickly communicate unavailability so a substitute can be found in time?
3. How do we onboard new volunteers with minimal friction?
4. How do we keep the rota fair so no one carries a disproportionate load?

---

## 🚩 Problem Statement

**Barrier duty** is a safety-critical volunteer role at a school. Parent volunteers control a pedestrian crossing barrier for 30-minute slots during school drop-off and pick-up. The current coordination relies heavily on a single admin sending WhatsApp/email messages to fill gaps, which is slow and error-prone.

### Pain Points
- Volunteers forget their shifts → no-shows → children cross unsafely
- Admin spends hours per week on manual chasing
- No single source of truth for who is on duty when
- New volunteers don't know where to start
